## 2. Customer Interview Script (Structured Questionnaire)
*Rule: Do not pitch features. Ask about current operational pain, energy downtime, and financial costs.*

### Section A: Baseline Energy Infrastructure
1. "What is your continuous electrical load (in Watts) at remote or unmanned monitoring locations?"
2. "What power source do you currently use (Solar PV + lead-acid/lithium, diesel generator, or grid)?"
3. "During the monsoon season (June to September) or extended overcast periods, how many hours of battery autonomy do you typically achieve before blackout?"

### Section B: The Cost of Energy Downtime
4. "When a remote station drops offline due to low battery, what is the operational cost (technician site visit, lost telemetry, SLA penalty)?"
5. "What is your fully-loaded monthly cost per site for fuel, battery replacement, and technician maintenance visits?"

### Section C: The Hybrid Wind Solution
6. "If a compact, silent micro-turbine could generate continuous 50W–200W throughout monsoon storms and night hours, shrinking your required battery bank size by 50%, what would that be worth to your operations?"
7. "What are your primary technical hesitations regarding wind hardware (noise, rooftop vibration, maintenance, storm damage)?"
8. "What budget range is approved at a departmental level without issuing a formal multi-vendor RFP (e.g. ₹35,000 to ₹50,000)?"

### Section D: Pilot Commitment
9. "If we deploy a fully instrumented, lab-validated pilot unit on your site for 30 days under a performance guarantee, would you be willing to sign an evaluation agreement and provide operational feedback?"

## 3. Pilot Deployment Letter of Intent (LOI) / MoU Framework
*Key terms to include in the 1-page pilot agreement:*
- **Equipment Provided:** One Pawan Shakti 1.0 Micro-Turbine (1 m² swept area) + Guyed Mast (3–6 m) + TCCB Controller + 24V Battery Pack.
- **Duration:** 30 to 60 Days continuous field evaluation.
- **Provider Obligations (Pawan Shakti):** Complete mechanical installation, safety verification, real-time telemetry dashboard access, full insurance liability.
- **Host Customer Obligations:** Designated mounting access, permission for anemometer and electrical load connection, written feedback upon hitting power target.
- **Success Criteria:** Turbine achieves uninterrupted operation with $\ge 95\%$ uptime and generates documented energy during night/monsoon periods without exceeding 45 dBA noise.
